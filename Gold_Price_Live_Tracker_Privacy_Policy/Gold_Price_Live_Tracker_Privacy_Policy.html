Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

Gold Price Live India Tracker is a free app that shows live gold and silver rates across Indian cities, along with tools like rate charts, a purity calculator, price alerts, and a personal portfolio tracker. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

Information We Collect

Gold Price Live India Tracker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app does not require an account or login. All of your preferences and data stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Personal Data

We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Payment Information

Your Preferences and Portfolio Data

Your selected city, purity, currency, language, price alerts, and any gold holdings you add to the Portfolio Tracker (weight, purchase price, date, and notes) are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. This data is never uploaded, backed up, or transmitted to us or to any server, and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage.

Live Rate and Currency Data

To show live gold and silver rates, the app fetches publicly published rate pages from goodreturns.in, and to convert prices to other currencies it fetches exchange rates from open.er-api.com. These are one-way requests to retrieve public rate data — no personal information or device identifiers are sent as part of these requests.

Data Sharing

Gold Price Live India Tracker does not share any user information with third parties. If you use the app's "Share" feature to share a rate chart, the image or text is sent directly to the app you pick (such as WhatsApp, email, or messaging) using your device's native share sheet, and is not routed through us.

Advertisements

Gold Price Live India Tracker does not display advertisements.

Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. It fetches gold, silver, and currency rate data from public sources (goodreturns.in and open.er-api.com) purely to display live prices in the app.

Permissions

The application requests the following permission:

  • Internet access — to fetch live gold, silver, and currency rates

No sensitive device permissions, such as camera, location, or contacts, are requested or used.

Cookies

Gold Price Live India Tracker does not use cookies.

Children's Privacy

Gold Price Live India Tracker is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no user data stored on our servers. Your preferences and portfolio data remain under your control on your own device at all times.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
