Delete Your AquaRush Account and Data

This page explains how to delete your account and data in AquaRush: Monsoon Courier, a game by Techseek.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open AquaRush and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile screen.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

You are signed out on all devices straight away.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer use the app, send an email to mygenzyno@gmail.com with the subject "AquaRush account deletion". Send it from the email address you used to register, and include your username. We may ask you to confirm the request before we delete the account.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Email address, username and passwordDeleted
Profile, level, XP and statsDeleted
Game results and run historyDeleted
Leaderboard entriesRemoved
Coins, coin history, missions, daily rewards and cosmetic itemsDeleted
Sign-in sessionsEnded immediately and deleted
Security records linked to your accountDeleted

Your account and data are permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days of your request, including the security records linked to your account. Deletion cannot be undone, and deleted coins and items cannot be restored.

Guest players

If you play as a guest, nothing is stored on our servers. To remove guest data, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app.
