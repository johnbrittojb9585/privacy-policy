Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

AquaRush: Monsoon Courier ("AquaRush", "the game", "we") is a free endless-runner game. You ride a waterboard through a flooded city, dodge obstacles, collect coins and packages, complete missions, unlock cosmetic items and compete on leaderboards. The game is available on Android and in a web browser. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game handles and how.

Guest Mode and Accounts

You can play AquaRush as a guest without creating an account. In guest mode the game runs entirely on your device, and nothing is sent to our servers. Your settings and progress stay only on your device.

If you choose to create an account, we collect the information described below. You need an account to appear on leaderboards and to keep your progress, coins and items on our server.

Information We Collect

Only if you create an account and sign in:

InformationWhy we use it
Email addressTo create your account and let you sign in. It is not shown to other players
UsernameYour player name. It is shown to other players on the leaderboards
PasswordTo protect your account. Passwords are stored only as a salted one-way hash (BCrypt), never in plain text
Game resultsFor each run: score, distance, coins, packages and energy collected, power-ups used, run duration and a log of in-game events. We use these to check that results are fair, grant rewards and rank players
Progress and inventoryLevel, XP, best score, totals, in-game coin balance and its history, missions, daily reward streak, and the cosmetic items you own and have equipped
Security recordsA record of certain account events (for example, registration, account deletion requests, item purchases and suspicious sign-in activity), linked to your account ID, to protect the service and investigate abuse

When you sign in or register, your device's IP address is used temporarily to limit repeated sign-in attempts. It is held in memory only and is not saved with your account.

We do not collect your real name, location, contacts, phone number, photos, camera or microphone data, payment information, or advertising identifiers.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Settings such as music and sound volume, vibration, graphics quality and tutorial progress
  • Unsent runs: if you play while signed in but offline, the finished run is saved on your device and uploaded when you are back online
  • Sign-in tokens, kept in your device's secure storage so you stay signed in

On Android this information is stored in the app's private storage. In the web version it is stored in your browser's storage.

How We Use Information

  • To provide sign-in and keep your progress, coins and items on your account
  • To verify game results, grant rewards and run missions and daily rewards
  • To show global and weekly leaderboards
  • To keep the service secure and prevent cheating and abuse (for example, rate limiting repeated sign-in attempts and flagging suspicious results)

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Information Other Players Can See

If you have an account, your username, scores and rank may appear on the leaderboards, which other players can see. Your email address is never shown.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except our server and database hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

In-Game Currency and Purchases

Coins are earned only by playing and can be spent only on cosmetic items. AquaRush has no real-money purchases and does not collect any payment information.

Advertisements and Analytics

AquaRush does not display advertisements and does not use third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking services.

Data Storage and Security

Account data is stored on a server and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed, and sign-in tokens are stored on our server only as hashes. Sign-in tokens change regularly, and if an old token is ever reused, all of your sessions are signed out as a precaution. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Permissions

  • Internet: to sign in, upload game results and load leaderboards, missions and the shop
  • Network state: to detect when you are back online so unsent runs can be uploaded
  • Vibrate: to give haptic feedback during play (you can turn this off in Settings)

Cookies

The game does not use cookies. The web version uses your browser's local storage only for the on-device information described above.

Your Choices: Access and Deletion

You can view your profile, stats and run history in the game. If you have an account, you can delete it from the Profile screen. When you request deletion, you are signed out on all devices straight away, and your account and the data linked to it are permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or request deletion by contacting us at the address below.

Guest data stays on your device and is removed by clearing the app's storage, clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app.

Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and associated data are deleted as described above. Expired sign-in tokens are removed automatically. Limited security records may be kept for a short period for abuse prevention.

Children's Privacy

AquaRush is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
