Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Kolam Master ("the app", "we") is a free app for learning, drawing and celebrating traditional kolams (rangoli). You can browse designs, follow step-by-step tutorials, draw in a studio, trace designs with your camera, take part in daily challenges and keep track of your progress. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Guest Mode and Accounts

You can use Kolam Master as a guest without creating an account. In guest mode, your saved kolams, drawings, settings and progress stay only on your device.

If you choose to create an account, we collect the information described below so that you can sign in and keep your progress.

Information We Collect

Only if you create an account and sign in:

InformationWhy we use it
NameTo personalise the app (for example, your greeting)
Username (or email address)To create your account and let you sign in. You can choose a username; an email address is optional
PasswordTo protect your account. Passwords are stored only as a salted one-way hash (BCrypt), never in plain text
Progress and activityPoints, badges, streaks, completed tutorials and challenges, saved kolams and settings, so they can be restored on another device
Your drawingsDrawings you create in the studio, if you sync them to your account

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, payment information, or advertising identifiers.

Photos and Camera

The camera and photo picker are used only for the features you choose: tracing a kolam over the camera view, and the "Photo to Kolam" tool. Photos are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

How We Use Information

  • To provide sign-in and keep your progress, saved kolams and drawings in sync
  • To show daily kolams, challenges and festival content
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (for example, rate limiting repeated login attempts)

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except our server hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf. If you use the app's "Share" or "Download" features, the image is sent directly to the app you choose through your device's share sheet, and is not routed through us.

Advertisements and Analytics

Kolam Master does not display advertisements and does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Data Storage and Security

Account data is stored on a server and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage. Passwords are hashed. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can show reminders (such as a daily kolam reminder) on your device. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

Permissions

  • Camera: to trace a kolam over the camera view and to take a photo for Photo to Kolam
  • Internet: to sign in, sync your progress and load daily content
  • Notifications: to show reminders you have enabled
  • Run at startup / Vibrate: to restore your reminders after a device restart and to give haptic feedback

Cookies

The app does not use cookies.

Your Choices: Access, Export and Deletion

If you have an account you can, in the app's Settings, export your data and delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your account and the data linked to it from our servers. You can also request deletion without the app; see how to delete your account and data. Guest data stays on your device and is removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and associated data are deleted from our active systems. Limited security logs may be kept for a short period for abuse prevention.

Children's Privacy

Kolam Master is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
