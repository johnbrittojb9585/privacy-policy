Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

AI Photo Editor is a free photo editing app that lets you edit photos, apply filters and adjustments, use AI-powered tools such as background removal, object removal and enhancement, and create collages, frames and text overlays. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

Information We Collect

AI Photo Editor does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app works completely on your device. Your photos, edits, AI tool results, and projects are processed locally and are never sent to any server.

Personal Data

We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Payment Information

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Your Photos and Projects

Photos you open, edit, or create are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, backed up, or transmitted to us or to any server. AI tools, filters, and all other edits are applied on your device. Your projects and app settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device.

Data Sharing

AI Photo Editor does not share any user information with third parties. If you choose to use the app's "Share" feature, the selected image is sent directly to the app you pick (such as email or messaging) using your device's native share sheet, and is not routed through us. If you choose to save an edited photo, it is saved to your device's photo gallery.

Advertisements

AI Photo Editor does not display advertisements.

Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If you choose to send feedback from the app's Settings screen, a Google Form opens in your web browser. Any information you enter in that form is provided voluntarily and is handled by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The application requests the following permissions, used only to perform the action you request:

  • Camera — to take a photo to edit
  • Photo Library — to choose photos to edit and to save your edited photos
  • Storage — to save edited photos on older Android versions (Android 10 and below)
  • Internet — required by the app framework; your photos are not sent over the internet

No sensitive device permissions are used to collect personal information.

Cookies

AI Photo Editor does not use cookies.

Children's Privacy

AI Photo Editor is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

Because all of your data stays on your device, you are always in control of it. You can delete individual projects inside the app, or remove all app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Photos you saved to your gallery remain there until you delete them.

Security

Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no user data stored on our servers. Your photos and projects remain under your control on your own device at all times.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
