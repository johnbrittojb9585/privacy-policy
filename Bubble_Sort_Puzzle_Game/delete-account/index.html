Delete Your Bubble Sort Puzzle Account

This page explains how to delete your Bubble Sort Puzzle account (developer: TechSeek Apps) and the data linked to it. You can delete it yourself inside the app, or ask us to delete it by email if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1: Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Bubble Sort Puzzle and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button on the home screen.
  3. In the Account card at the top, tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account and all of its data are deleted from our servers straight away, and the app returns to the sign-in screen.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to mygenzyno@gmail.com with the subject "Bubble Sort Puzzle - Account deletion request" and include:

  • your username, and
  • the email address on the account (if you added one). Please send the request from that email address so we can confirm the account is yours.

We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days. We will reply by email once it is done. If we cannot verify that you own the account, we may ask you to delete it from inside the app instead.

What is deleted

Data What happens
Username, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash) Permanently deleted
Account ID and the app installation ID linked to the account Permanently deleted
Game progress: unlocked levels, level results, stars, best moves and times Permanently deleted
Coins, unlocked themes, daily challenge attempts and streaks Permanently deleted
Progress saved on your device Erased when you delete in the app. If you delete by email, uninstall the app or use Settings > Reset Progress.

What is kept

We do not keep any of your account data after deletion. Copies in routine server backups are overwritten as those backups expire, within 90 days. Bubble Sort Puzzle has no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics, so no other company holds data about your account.

Guest players

If you only played as a guest, no username, email or password was ever collected. Your progress is linked only to a random installation ID. To remove it from your device, uninstall the app or use Settings > Reset Progress.
