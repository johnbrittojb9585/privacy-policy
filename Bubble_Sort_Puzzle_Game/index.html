Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Bubble Sort Puzzle ("the app", "we") is a free colour-sorting puzzle game. You sort coloured bubbles into tubes across 1,000 levels, play a daily challenge, and try AI Mode (endless puzzles). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Guest Play and Accounts

You can play as a guest without creating an account. As a guest, the app creates a random anonymous player ID on your device so your progress can be saved and backed up. You can play offline; the app syncs when a connection is available.

If you choose to create an account, you can sign in with your username or email address and password to keep your progress on any device. Progress you made as a guest on that device is moved into the new account.

Information We Collect

Information Why we use it
Anonymous player ID (a random value generated on your device; our server stores only a one-way hash of it) To recognise your game and keep your progress and coins separate from other players
Account details, only if you create an account: username, email address (optional) and password To let you sign in and restore your progress on another device. Passwords are stored only as a salted one-way hash (BCrypt), never in plain text. Your email is used only for signing in
Game progress: levels completed, stars, move counts, time taken, hints used, coin balance, daily challenge results, achievements, AI Mode streak and skill rating To save and restore your progress, award coins and stars, and prevent coins from being awarded twice
Device platform (for example Android or Web) and app version To keep the service compatible and to fix bugs
Technical logs: IP address, request time and a random request ID To keep the service secure, limit abusive traffic and diagnose errors. Logs are kept for a limited period
Your settings (sound, vibration, colour-blind mode, reduced motion, high contrast, theme, Smart Assist) Stored on your device so the app looks and behaves the way you chose

We do not collect your real name, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, files, or payment information. Guests provide no email address or password.

On-Device AI Features

The AI coach, Smart Assist, AI Solve and AI Mode run entirely on your device using search and heuristic algorithms. They are not a cloud service or a large language model. Your board, moves and skill rating are not sent anywhere to produce hints or puzzles. Your skill rating is stored in your save so AI Mode can match your level.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the game, save and sync your progress, and award coins and stars
  • To show the daily challenge and remote game settings
  • To keep the service secure and prevent cheating and abuse (for example, rate limiting)
  • To find and fix bugs and improve level difficulty

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Advertisements

Bubble Sort Puzzle does not display advertisements and contains no advertising SDKs. We do not collect or use your advertising ID. Hints, extra tubes and AI Solve are paid for only with coins you earn by playing.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only with our server hosting and database provider, which stores it on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

Analytics

The app does not use third-party analytics or tracking services to profile you. Basic gameplay events (such as level completed) are used only to understand game balance.

Data Storage and Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Your anonymous ID is stored on our servers only as a hash, and sessions use short-lived signed tokens. Local progress is stored on your device with an automatic backup copy. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Permissions

  • Internet: to sync progress and load the daily challenge
  • Vibrate (optional): for haptic feedback when you move bubbles

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or storage.

Cookies

The app does not use cookies. (The web version stores your save in the browser's local storage.)

Your Choices: Access and Deletion

You can clear all data stored on your device at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of your account or of the progress we hold on our servers, email us at the address below with the subject "Delete my data", including your username (for accounts) or, for guests, sending from the device you play on. We will delete your account, player record and associated progress and coin data within 30 days. You may also ask us for a copy of the data linked to your account or player ID.

Data Retention

We keep your game data for as long as your player record exists. Technical logs are kept for a short period for security and debugging, then deleted. Records inactive for a long period may be deleted.

Children's Privacy

Bubble Sort Puzzle is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Guest play collects no email or contact details from guests. If you believe a child's personal information has been provided to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If we make material changes, we will also notify you in the app.
