Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Bubble Sort Puzzle ("the app", "we") is a free colour-sorting puzzle game. You sort coloured bubbles into tubes across 1,000 levels, play a daily challenge, and try AI Mode (endless puzzles). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Guest Play, No Account Needed

Bubble Sort Puzzle does not ask you to register. There is no name, email address, phone number or password. When you first open the app it creates a random anonymous player ID on your device so your progress can be saved and backed up. You can play offline; the app syncs when a connection is available.

Information We Collect

InformationWhy we use it
Anonymous player ID (a random value generated on your device; our server stores only a one-way hash of it)To recognise your game and keep your progress and coins separate from other players
Game progress: levels completed, stars, move counts, time taken, hints used, coin balance, daily challenge results, achievements, AI Mode streak and skill ratingTo save and restore your progress, award coins and stars, and prevent a reward from being paid twice
Rewarded-ad activity (which reward you chose and when)To grant rewards you earned and to enforce fair daily limits
Device platform (for example Android or Web) and app versionTo keep the service compatible and to fix bugs
Technical logs: IP address, request time and a random request IDTo keep the service secure, limit abusive traffic and diagnose errors. Logs are kept for a limited period
Your settings (sound, vibration, colour-blind mode, reduced motion, high contrast, theme, Smart Assist)Stored on your device so the app looks and behaves the way you chose

We do not collect your name, email address, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, files, or payment information.

On-Device AI Features

The AI coach, Smart Assist, AI Solve and AI Mode run entirely on your device using search and heuristic algorithms. They are not a cloud service or a large language model. Your board, moves and skill rating are not sent anywhere to produce hints or puzzles. Your skill rating is stored in your save so AI Mode can match your level.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the game, save and sync your progress, and award coins and stars
  • To show the daily challenge and remote game settings
  • To keep the service secure and prevent cheating and abuse (for example, rate limiting)
  • To find and fix bugs and improve level difficulty

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Advertisements

Bubble Sort Puzzle offers only optional rewarded ads: you may choose to watch an ad in exchange for a hint, an extra tube, an AI Solve, or bonus coins. We show no forced pop-up ads, and you can always use coins instead. If an ad network is enabled in your version of the app, that network may collect or use an advertising ID and device information under its own privacy policy. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Settings > Google > Ads.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only with our server hosting and database provider, which stores it on our behalf, and with the ad network described above if you choose to watch a rewarded ad. We may disclose information if required by law.

Analytics

The app does not use third-party analytics or tracking services to profile you. Basic gameplay events (such as level completed) are used only to understand game balance.

Data Storage and Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Your anonymous ID is stored on our servers only as a hash, and sessions use short-lived signed tokens. Local progress is stored on your device with an automatic backup copy. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Permissions

  • Internet: to sync progress, load the daily challenge and show rewarded ads
  • Vibrate (optional): for haptic feedback when you move bubbles

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or storage.

Cookies

The app does not use cookies. (The web version stores your save in the browser's local storage.)

Your Choices: Access and Deletion

You can clear all data stored on your device at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of the progress we hold on our servers, email us at the address below from the device you play on, with the subject "Delete my data" and, if possible, your player ID. We will delete your player record and associated progress, coin and reward data within 30 days. You may also ask us for a copy of the data linked to your player ID.

Data Retention

We keep your game data for as long as your player record exists. Technical logs are kept for a short period for security and debugging, then deleted. Records inactive for a long period may be deleted.

Children's Privacy

Bubble Sort Puzzle is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app collects no name, email or contact details from anyone. If you believe a child's personal information has been provided to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If we make material changes, we will also notify you in the app.
