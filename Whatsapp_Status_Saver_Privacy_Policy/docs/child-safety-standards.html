Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

These standards apply to Status Saver, an Android app for viewing and saving WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business statuses that you have already viewed on your phone, and for keeping profile pictures (DPs) that you choose from your own gallery. They set out our position on child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), how to report a concern, and what we do when we receive a report.

Status Saver is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta.

Our Commitment: Zero Tolerance

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation in any form. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), the sexualisation of minors, grooming, sextortion, and the trafficking of children.

Using Status Saver to view, save, store, share or distribute child sexual abuse material, or to exploit or endanger a child in any way, is strictly prohibited and is against our Terms & Conditions. Such content is also illegal, and we will cooperate fully with law enforcement.

How the App Works

To explain what we can and cannot do, it helps to know how Status Saver is built:

  • There are no accounts, profiles, chats, comments, feeds or public posts. Users cannot contact or find other users through the app.
  • Users cannot upload or publish content to the app or to us. The app only reads statuses already on the user's own device and saves copies to that device's gallery.
  • The app does not request internet access and has no servers. Media never passes through us, so we cannot see, scan or remove content on a user's device.
  • If a user shares a saved file, it goes directly to the app they choose (such as a messaging app) using Android's share sheet, and is then subject to that app's own safety rules.

Statuses come from WhatsApp. If you see child sexual abuse material in a status or chat, please also report it to WhatsApp directly, because WhatsApp can act on the account that posted it.

How to Report a Concern

If you believe Status Saver is being used to harm a child, or you have any child safety concern about the app, please contact us:

  • In the app: open Settings › Help & troubleshooting › Report a problem (also in the menu under Help & support) and describe the concern.
  • By email: mygenzyno@gmail.com, with the subject line "Child Safety Report".

Please describe what you saw and where. Do not attach, download or forward any abusive images or videos, even to report them, because possessing or sharing this material is itself illegal.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first.

You can also report child sexual abuse material directly to the authorities:

  • India: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, cybercrime.gov.in, or helpline 1930
  • United States and internationally: NCMEC CyberTipline, report.cybertip.org
  • United Kingdom: Internet Watch Foundation, report.iwf.org.uk
  • Other countries: find your national hotline through INHOPE, inhope.org

What We Do When We Receive a Report

  • We review every child safety report as a priority.
  • Where we have knowledge of child sexual abuse material or a child at risk, we report it to the relevant regional and national authorities, including the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal in India and the NCMEC CyberTipline, as required by applicable law.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests related to child safety. Because the app collects no personal data and stores nothing on our servers, we usually hold no user information to provide.
  • If a report shows the app itself could be misused to harm children, we will update the app to address it.

Compliance With Laws

We comply with all child safety laws that apply to us, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 in India, and we follow the Google Play child safety standards policy.
