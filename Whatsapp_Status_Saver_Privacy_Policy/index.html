Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Status Saver is a free Android app for viewing and saving WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business statuses that you have already viewed on your phone, and for keeping profile pictures (DPs) that you choose from your own gallery. It also keeps an on-device list of what you save and mark as favorite. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

Status Saver is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta.

Information We Collect

Status Saver does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app does not require an account or login, and it does not request internet access. All of your media, history and preferences stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Personal Data

We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • WhatsApp chats or messages
  • Payment Information

Status Folder Access

To show your statuses, you choose the WhatsApp status folder (".Statuses") yourself, using Android's system folder picker. This gives the app read-only access to that one folder only. The app cannot see your chats, contacts, or any other files, and it never changes or deletes files in that folder.

WhatsApp only stores a status on your phone after you have viewed it, so the app can only show statuses you have already seen. You can remove the app's folder access at any time in Settings › Disconnect status folder.

To suggest the right folder, the app checks on your device whether WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is installed. This check never leaves your device.

Images You Choose to Import (DP Saver)

If you use DP Saver, you pick an image from your gallery with Android's photo picker. The app keeps a copy of that image in its private storage on your device so you can preview, save or share it. It is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the picture in the app removes the app's copy. DP Saver cannot download other people's profile pictures from WhatsApp.

Files You Save

When you save a status or a profile picture, an exact copy is written to your device's gallery, in Pictures/StatusSaver and Movies/StatusSaver. These are ordinary files on your phone that you can view, move or delete with your gallery app at any time.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app keeps a database on your device only. It may contain:

  • A list of files you saved or imported (file name, type, size, date, a content fingerprint used to avoid duplicates, and where the copy is stored)
  • Your favorites
  • Save history, including the reason if a save failed
  • Scan history (when the status folder was checked and how many files were found)
  • Your settings, such as theme and sort order, and the folder you connected

The app also keeps small preview thumbnails in its cache, which you can clear in Settings.

This data is never uploaded, backed up, or transmitted to us or to any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage removes it. Copies you saved to your gallery are separate files and stay until you delete them. Because this data never leaves your device, we cannot see it, back it up, or delete it for you.

Problem Reports

If you choose "Report a problem" or "Copy diagnostics", the app prepares a short text report to help solve the issue. It contains only the app version, Android version, phone model, whether the status folder is connected and its path, which WhatsApp apps are installed, and scan and save counts and times. It never includes your photos, videos, or file names.

The report is only sent if you send it yourself from your email app, or paste it somewhere you choose. You can read it before sending.

Network Use

The app does not request the internet permission and makes no network calls. Viewing, saving and organising statuses works fully offline. The only time another app goes online is when you choose an action that hands off to it, for example "Rate app" opening Google Play, or "Report a problem" opening your email app.

Data Sharing

Status Saver does not share any user information with third parties. If you use the app's "Share" feature, the selected image or video is sent directly to the app you pick (such as WhatsApp, email, or messaging) using your device's native share sheet, and is not routed through us.

Advertisements

Status Saver does not display advertisements and does not use an advertising ID.

Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. It is built with open-source libraries that run entirely on your device.

Permissions

The application uses the following access:

  • Folder access — read-only access to the status folder you choose through Android's folder picker, which you can remove at any time
  • Photo picker — only when you choose an image to import into DP Saver; no permission is needed for this
  • Storage — only on Android 9 and below, to save copies to your gallery

No other sensitive device permissions, such as internet, camera, location, contacts, microphone, or SMS, are requested or used.

Cookies

Status Saver does not use cookies.

Children's Privacy

Status Saver is intended for general audiences, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Responsibility

Please respect other people's privacy and copyright. Only save or share someone's status or picture if you have their permission.

Security

Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no user data stored on our servers. Your media, history and preferences remain under your control on your own device at all times, protected by your operating system's app sandboxing.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
