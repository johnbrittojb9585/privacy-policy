Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

Scan Pro OCR is a free document scanning app that lets you scan documents, save them as PDFs, and extract text using on-device OCR. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

Information We Collect

Scan Pro OCR does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app works completely on your device. Your scanned documents, PDFs, and extracted text are processed locally and are never sent to any server.

Personal Data

We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Payment Information

Your Documents and Scans

Photos, scans, and PDFs you create are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, backed up, or transmitted to us or to any server. Text extracted through OCR is generated and stored locally on your device.

Data Sharing

Scan Pro OCR does not share any user information with third parties. If you choose to use the app's "Share" feature, the selected file is sent directly to the app you pick (such as email or messaging) using your device's native share sheet, and is not routed through us.

Advertisements

Scan Pro OCR does not display advertisements.

Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. On-device text recognition is powered by Google ML Kit, which runs locally on your device and does not send your documents or images to Google or any other server.

Permissions

The application requests the following permissions, used only to perform the action you request:

  • Camera — to scan documents
  • Photo Library — to import existing images for scanning or OCR
  • Storage — to save PDFs and manage your scan history

No sensitive device permissions are used to collect personal information.

Cookies

Scan Pro OCR does not use cookies.

Children's Privacy

Scan Pro OCR is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no user data stored on our servers. Your scanned documents remain under your control on your own device at all times.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
