Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

TechSeek builds QR & Barcode Scanner ("the app") as a free app for scanning QR codes and barcodes and for creating QR codes for links, text, Wi-Fi, contacts and more. It also keeps an on-device history of what you scan and create. This Privacy Policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

In short: the app has no account system, it is supported by banner ads from Google AdMob, your scan and QR history stays on your device and is never shared with advertisers, and the few optional online features are off until you turn them on.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not require an account or login. We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Precise Location
  • Contacts
  • Payment Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. The advertising described in section 7 is handled by Google, not by us.

2. Camera

With your permission, the camera is used only to detect QR codes and barcodes in the live viewfinder. Frames are analysed on your device in real time. The camera feed is never recorded, saved as a photo, or transmitted anywhere. If you deny camera access you can still create QR codes and scan from an existing image.

3. Images You Choose to Scan

If you use "scan from image", the picture you select is read on your device to look for a code. It is not uploaded, copied, or kept by the app after decoding.

4. History Stored on Your Device

When you scan a code or create a QR code, the app saves an entry in a database on your device only. Each entry may include the decoded or encoded content, a short title, the code type and format, the date and time, and anything you add yourself: favourites, notes, tags, collections and batch scan sessions (names, quantities and notes). Your settings are also stored locally.

If you use product lookup, the product details returned are also saved on your device so they work offline. You can clear them in Settings › Data.

This data is never uploaded by the app. You can open or delete individual entries, clear all history, or export it from the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage removes this data. Because it never leaves your device, we cannot see it, back it up, or delete it for you.

5. Export and Import

Exporting history creates a JSON or CSV file that you then send or save wherever you choose, using your device's native share sheet. Once the file leaves the app, it is handled by the destination you picked, under that service's terms. Importing reads a file you select and adds its entries to your local history.

6. Barcode Recognition Technology

Code detection uses Google's on-device machine-learning libraries (ML Kit), which are bundled with the app. Image analysis for scanning runs entirely on your device; image data is not sent to Google for this purpose.

7. Advertisements

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address (used to estimate general location)
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system and app version)
  • Ad interactions (such as ads viewed and tapped) and diagnostic data

Google may use this information to show personalised ads where you allow it, and non-personalised ads otherwise. Your scan and QR history, the content of the codes you scan or create, and camera images are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › About › Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

8. Network Use and Optional Online Features

Apart from loading ads, the app makes no background network calls and uses no analytics. Scanning, creating QR codes, history, batch scanning, the on-device link safety check and the on-device AI assistant all work fully offline.

A connection is also made when you choose to act on a scanned code, for example tapping a link, email address or phone number, which hands off to your browser, mail or phone app, or when you use one of the optional online features below. Each is off until you turn it on, shows what it sends before the first use, and can be turned off in Settings › Online features.

  • Product lookup: sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, an open product database, to fetch product details.
  • Online link check (only in versions where it is offered): sends only the one link you choose to check to a link reputation service.
  • Cloud AI (only in versions where it is offered): sends the content of the one code you ask about, and your questions, to a cloud AI service. Wi-Fi passwords are always removed first, and personal details such as names, phone numbers and email addresses are removed unless you choose to include them.

9. Data Sharing

We do not share your history or the content of your codes with anyone. Data collected for advertising is handled by Google as described in section 7. If you use an optional online feature, only the data described in section 8 is sent. If you use the app's "Share" feature to share a QR image or scan result, the image or text is sent directly to the app you pick (such as WhatsApp, email, or messaging) using your device's native share sheet, and is not routed through us.

10. Permissions

The application requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: to scan QR codes and barcodes with the live camera
  • Photos / media: only when you pick an image to scan a code from
  • Vibrate: for the optional scan feedback buzz
  • Internet: to load ads and for the optional online features
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads

No other sensitive device permissions, such as precise location, contacts, microphone, SMS, or call logs, are requested or used.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The app itself does not use cookies. Google's advertising SDK may use device identifiers, such as the advertising ID, in a similar way to cookies to serve, limit and measure ads.

12. Children's Privacy

QR & Barcode Scanner is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

13. Security and Retention

Your history and preferences are stored in your device's app storage, protected by your operating system's app sandboxing, and kept until you delete them or uninstall the app. We do not keep copies, because we never receive them. Online requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS). AI conversations are kept only while the screen is open and are not saved.

14. Your Choices

  • Deny or revoke camera permission in your device settings.
  • Clear scan history and created QR codes from Settings › Data.
  • Export your history to keep your own copy.
  • Change ad consent in Settings › About › Ad privacy choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

15. Third-Party Services

  • Google ML Kit (on-device barcode recognition)
  • Google AdMob (banner ads)
  • Open Food Facts (optional product lookup; open database run by a non-profit, openfoodfacts.org)

ML Kit and AdMob are provided by Google LLC and are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. When you use an optional online feature, the service that receives the request handles it under its own privacy policy.
