Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

ClueVerse is a free detective mystery game for Android and the web. You investigate fictional cases, search scenes, question suspects, connect clues and accuse the culprit. To let your progress follow you to any device, the game uses an account and keeps your progress on our server. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game records, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Summary

ClueVerse collects only what it needs to run the game: your account details, your game progress and settings, and any feedback you choose to send. It has no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and no crash-reporting services. We do not sell your information. You can permanently delete your account and all of its data from within the app at any time.

Information You Provide

WhatWhy
Account details: the email address or username you sign up with, your detective name, and your password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash and is never kept in readable form. To create your account and sign you in.
Game progress: the cases you play, the evidence you find, the links and notes you write, your interviews, hints used, theories, accusations, scores, ranks and achievements. To run the game and save your progress so it follows you to any device.
Settings and preferences: partner style, reduce-motion, haptics and AI-wording choices (saved with your account), and your light or dark theme (saved on your device). To remember how you like to play.
Feedback you send: your message, its category, an optional star rating, the app version and the platform (for example, Android). To fix bugs and improve the game.

Information Collected Automatically

When the app talks to our server, the server records technical logs: your account ID, the type and address of the request, the time, the result, and your IP address as seen by our web server. These logs are used only to keep the service secure, diagnose problems and prevent abuse. They never contain your password, sign-in tokens, notes or typed messages.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves a sign-in token in your device's secure storage (the Android Keystore, or the browser's local storage on the web) so you stay signed in, and your theme choice. The visual evidence board also remembers where you placed your cards, on your device only. These items are removed when you sign out, delete your account, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used solely to provide, secure and improve the game: to sign you in, save and show your progress, work out scores, ranks and achievements, answer your feedback and prevent abuse. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

AI-Assisted Wording

When the server's AI feature is switched on and "AI-assisted wording" is enabled in your Settings, the game may send the text you type to a character or to the detective partner, together with the relevant case text, to an AI provider (Anthropic) so that replies can be re-worded. Your email, username, detective name and account ID are not sent. The game's rules decide what is true; the AI only changes the wording, and AI-assisted text is labelled. You can turn this off at any time in Settings. Please do not type personal information into the game's text boxes.

Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not ask for or record:

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Photos, media or files
  • Camera or microphone input
  • Payment or financial information
  • Any advertising identifier

Data Sharing

ClueVerse does not sell or rent your personal information and does not share it with third parties for their own purposes. Information is handled only by the service providers that help us run the game, for that purpose only:

  • Hosting and database providers that store the game's data on our behalf.
  • An AI provider (Anthropic), only as described in the AI-Assisted Wording section.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of players and the service.

Advertisements

ClueVerse does not display advertisements.

Third-Party Services

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. Its only network connections are to the ClueVerse server (cloudverseapi.pdfcherry.com) and, when you tap a link such as "Update now" or "Open the web version", to the page you chose to open.

Permissions

The Android app requests one permission:

  • Internet — to load cases and save your progress on the ClueVerse server

No notification, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo or storage permission is requested.

Cookies

ClueVerse does not use cookies. The web version keeps your sign-in token and theme choice in the browser's local storage, as described above.

Children's Privacy

ClueVerse is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all of its data at any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Deleting it removes your account, game progress, notes, settings, achievements and feedback from our database. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then removed. If you cannot open the app, see how to delete your account.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, sign-in tokens expire and are refreshed automatically, and access to the database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your Rights and Choices

You can see your account details in the app, change your password, turn off AI wording, send us a request through the feedback form, and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or erase your data and to object to or restrict certain processing; use the in-app options or contact us as described below.

International Use

Our servers may be located in a different country from yours. By using the game you understand that your information may be processed there.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and important changes will also be announced in the app.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, use Settings → Send feedback in the app, or the developer contact shown on the ClueVerse page on Google Play.
