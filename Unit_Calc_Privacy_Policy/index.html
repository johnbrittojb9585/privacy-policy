Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

Unit Calc is a free utility application that provides unit conversions and calculators for engineering, finance, health, and everyday use. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

Information We Collect

Unit Calc does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information you enter into the app. All conversions and calculations are processed locally on your device and are never sent to us or to any server we control.

However, the app displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect certain information as described below.

Personal Data

We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or Files
  • Payment Information

Advertisements

Unit Calc displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support the free use of the app. AdMob may collect and use device information — including your advertising ID, IP address, and general device/usage data — to serve and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud.

You can review how Google collects and uses this data here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's own privacy policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Depending on your device and region, you can opt out of personalised advertising through your device's ad settings (for example, Android Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalisation) or through Google Ads Settings.

Data Sharing

Aside from the advertising data described above, which is collected directly by Google AdMob and not by us, Unit Calc does not share any user information with third parties.

Third-Party Services

This application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party service operated by Google and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use any other third-party analytics or tracking services.

Permissions

The application requests only the minimum permissions required for normal operation, including internet access (required to load ads and, where used, currency exchange rates) and the advertising ID permission required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve ads. No sensitive device permissions are used to collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Unit Calc is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown in this app are not targeted at children.

Security

Since no personal information you enter into the app is collected or transmitted by us, there is no user-entered data stored on our servers. Any data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's own security and privacy practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
